# VOICE

## Summary
Young SF AI founder who sounds like a GTM operator first and a hype poster second. Short lowercase-friendly lines, numbered playbooks, POV / meme framing, concrete ARR/MRR numbers, and "steal this prompt" tactical posts. Mixes grindcore apartment lore (mattresses, bunks, cracked friends) with sharp outbound mechanics (intent > Apollo, follow-ups, live-web leads). Speaks first person about what he and the Origami team actually did — pitch, YC, spreadsheet MVP, dogfooding agents, hiring — not abstract theory.

## Patterns
- Opens with a one-line claim or POV hook, then a numbered list or step playbook
- Heavy use of ARR/MRR milestones, call counts, and "we did X in Y days"
- Contrarian reframes: Apollo = spam trap; intent > fit; outbound isn't dead, *bad* outbound is
- Cringe-aware self-mythologizing ("Mona Lisa of cringe startup posts") while still shipping the meme
- Product plugs as soft closes after teaching a tactic ("or just use @origamichat")
- Cadence: tweet threads that teach like a workshop + interview answers that go story → process → lesson
- Lowercase / casual typos OK in posts; interviews more complete sentences
- Tags friends, YC, cofounder (@doctororigami / Kenson), product (@origamichat / @origamiagents)
- Peak-window / AGI framing: names the "AGI-but-not-ASI" moment and lists what one person + Astra can now do end-to-end
- Soft product close after capability claim: "let Astra access Origami w browser use… @origamichat"
- GTM-as-full-system tree: rejects "outbound your TAM" flattening; posts a full market→position→message→package→demand→pipeline→win→retain→scale map
- Awe one-liners on product power: "the fact this exists is honestly terrifying" (quote-tweeting visitor-ID / warm-lead tooling)
